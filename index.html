Understanding Yemen. Informing the region’s future.

YACSRS is a U.S.-based independent research initiative producing rigorous, accessible analysis on Yemen, regional security, governance, economic development, and the strategic environment surrounding the Red Sea and Arabian Peninsula.

Our Focus

Research grounded in evidence and regional understanding

The Center connects Yemeni knowledge with American research standards to help policymakers, scholars, journalists, and the public understand complex developments.

01

Yemen Political Affairs

Institutions, political actors, peace efforts, governance, state-building, and the evolution of Yemen’s political order.

02

Regional Security

Strategic competition, conflict dynamics, defense policy, cross-border risks, and the security architecture of the Arabian Peninsula.

03

Red Sea & Maritime Strategy

Bab al-Mandab, shipping security, ports, regional trade, maritime threats, and the geopolitical importance of the Red Sea corridor.

04

Governance & Development

Public institutions, infrastructure, economic recovery, energy, humanitarian transition, and practical pathways toward resilient development.

05

U.S.–Yemen Relations

Diplomatic engagement, security cooperation, diaspora perspectives, aid policy, and opportunities for constructive bilateral relations.

06

Data & Strategic Foresight

Timelines, indicators, scenario analysis, visual explainers, and structured data that turn fragmented information into usable insight.

Why YACSRS

A bridge between local knowledge and international policy

Yemen is often discussed through incomplete data, political narratives, or distant interpretation. YACSRS aims to combine firsthand regional understanding with disciplined, verifiable research.

Our purpose is not to promote a faction. It is to improve the quality of decisions made about Yemen and the wider region.

IndependenceResearch conclusions are not dictated by political or financial interests.
AccuracyClaims should be sourced, qualified, corrected, and separated from opinion.
AccessibilityComplex issues should be understandable to decision-makers and the public.
Regional RespectAnalysis should reflect Yemen’s diversity, history, and human realities.
