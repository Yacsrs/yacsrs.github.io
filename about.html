About YACSRS

An independent platform for serious regional research

Our Mission

To produce independent, evidence-based research that improves understanding of Yemen and its regional environment and supports informed, practical, and peaceful policy choices.

Our Vision

To become a trusted Yemeni-American source of strategic analysis connecting local knowledge, rigorous research, and constructive engagement with policymakers, academics, journalists, civil society, and the wider public.

What makes the Center different

YACSRS is designed to combine Yemeni perspective with international research standards. Its work will emphasize verifiable evidence, transparent methodology, bilingual accessibility, data visualization, and policy relevance.

Launch-stage transparency: YACSRS is currently an emerging online research initiative. It does not yet maintain a physical office, permanent staff, or government affiliation. This statement should remain visible until those facts change.
Core Principles

How we intend to work

Editorial Independence

Authors retain analytical freedom, and funding sources must not control findings or recommendations.

Research Integrity

Publications should explain sources, limitations, uncertainties, and the distinction between fact and interpretation.

Nonpartisanship

The Center will evaluate policies and actors without serving as a communications platform for any faction.
