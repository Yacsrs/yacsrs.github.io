Research Method

Minimum publication standard

Every substantial YACSRS publication should include an executive summary, key findings, methodology, sources, limitations, policy implications, author biography, publication date, and a correction contact.

Major reports should receive external review from at least one knowledgeable person who was not involved in writing the paper. Opinion articles should be clearly labeled and should never be presented as institutional research.
